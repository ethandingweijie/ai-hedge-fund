## What D4(b) (moat ROIC without goodwill) did
- 79 of 621 valued names moved more than 0.5%; median move among them **+3.1%** (none moved down materially).
- Largest: CHTR +19%, S63.SI +16%, T +13%, BS6.SI +10%, MDLZ +9%, IBKR +9%, HRL +9%, STZ +8%, CDW +8%, CCEP +8%.
- It did **not** reduce the range-only count (227 → 229): a moat tag lifts the terminal value by up to ~18%, which
  moves a DCF leg by single digits -- not enough to close method gaps whose median is 4x.

## Reading
- The 4x bridge halves the range-only list (227 → 113) as a presentation change; the Neutral rating cap above 2.5x
  is unchanged.
- The remaining gap is the low cash-flow legs (DCF / EPV / FCF Yield / DDM) against multiples -- i.e. the growth
  fade (D4 c) and how those legs capitalise mature cash flows, not the moat test. D4(c) base rates are the next lever.
