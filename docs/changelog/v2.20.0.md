## 2026-09-20 to 2026-09-23 — v2.20.0: Dynamic Multiples, Waves 2 and 3 (Power & Transition, Aerospace & Defence), and an Explicit Unrated State

### Executive Summary
The closed loop was run on power and transition (Wave 2) and aerospace and defence (Wave 3), and the multiples those waves stand on stopped being fixed numbers. A quarterly engine now proposes each industry's through-cycle multiple from its own history, the normalised legs price on it, and forward (NTM) peer medians are measured every week. Names the engine cannot value are now published as Unrated with no figure, instead of a fractional-weight number that gets quoted. Neither wave cleared the consensus bar outright; every miss is attributed in its scorecard, and the AI-power re-rating is recorded as a regime rather than tuned away.
