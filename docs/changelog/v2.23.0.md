## 2026-10-03 — v2.23.0: One SOTP Format, the Fast Research Tier, and the Guidance → Estimates Channel

### Executive Summary
Every sum-of-the-parts leg now publishes in the one analyst format (segment table, then the bridge to a per-share figure). Deep research moved to the DashScope flash tier for speed, with the thinking it does spend pointed at one job: translating management guidance into the bear / base / bull estimates a valuation runs on. Those estimates set the DCF's first two years per scenario and are shown on the web, in the PDF and in the Excel model, for every sector and profile.

### One format for every SOTP leg
- 71038af: SOTP (analyst), SOTP (segments), SOTP / NAV (look-through), SOTP (published) and SOTP (FRE + carry) all render as the analyst card: valuation sentence, segment table (forward revenue, earnings, method, multiple, value, share), then associates, net cash, holdco discount and the per-share figure. One `sotp_breakdown` block per run, built from whichever SOTP leg carries weight, with that leg's method and weight in the title; the look-through and published legs now trace their own tables so the block is their numbers. The segment-only panel and PDF block are retired; archived runs' legacy blocks are adapted on read.

### The fast research tier (objective i)
- `qwen3.8-flash` is the research default for Tier-1 search, synthesis and every extractor (the owner's "Qwen3.8-Flash-Next"; the id that resolves on this account carries no suffix). `DEEP_RESEARCH_MODEL` and `DEEP_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS_MODEL` still override — production sets both, so the switch takes effect there only when those variables change.
- The flash tier thinks by default on the Anthropic-compatible endpoint: with no thinking parameter and a 4,000-token cap it returned a thinking block and no text. Every client deep research builds now carries a thinking policy (off unless a budget is asked for), and the Tier-1 search runs with thinking off under the "pro" strategy (the flash tier refuses "agent"): measured 5 s against 104 s for one guidance question. `DEEP_RESEARCH_SEARCH_STRATEGY` and `DEEP_RESEARCH_SEARCH_THINKING=on` restore the old behaviour.
- Report length: a compact budget (3,000 words of prose outside the machine-read blocks, per-section caps, no introductions) and a 12,000-token output cap on the flash tier. `DEEP_RESEARCH_LENGTH=full` and `DEEP_RESEARCH_REPORT_MAX_TOKENS` restore the long form.

### Guidance → estimates (objective ii)
- The search sequence leads with guidance: the latest results release and its ranges, the call's commentary and medium-term targets, consensus for the comparison. A new section 2G carries a machine-readable GUIDANCE_BLOCK (what management guided, consensus, the prior-year actual), the arithmetic of what it implies, management's record on its own guidance, and the analyst's recommended FY+1 / FY+2 revenue growth, margin and EPS for bear / base / bull with a confidence. Both section parsers (live and archive) read 2A–2G.
- A universal `guidance_estimates` extractor runs over 2G with a thinking budget (6,000 tokens; the only extractor that thinks) and returns rates as decimals, so the engine needs no currency or scale conversion. Persisted extractor sets from before 2G are reused for the extractors they have; only the missing one runs.
- The DCF's guidance channel: years 1–2 of each scenario's growth come from that scenario's estimate (FY+1, then FY+2), a three-year fade lands on the engine's own path by year 6, and the terminal is untouched. LOW-confidence estimates are shown but not channelled; `GUIDANCE_CHANNEL=off` disables. Constants are PROPOSED in `valuation_constants.json["guidance_channel"]` for the owner's review. This closes the gap where guided growth was overridden by analyst dispersion bands before the scenarios ran.

### Estimates on every surface (objective iii)
- Web (desktop and mobile paths): a "Management guidance → estimates" card beside the SOTP card — guidance as stated, consensus, the model's bear / base / bull, the base DCF's growth path, and whether it was applied or why not.
- PDF: the same block after the SOTP block in the Valuation Summary. Excel: a "Guidance" tab with the same figures and the year-by-year growth path the base DCF ran on.

### The PM write-up, to an institutional standard
- Owner's critique (2026-10-03), five findings, each now a rule in the rationale prompt: no engine residue (flags, gates, legs and runs are translated into operating reality), one price anchor (the 12-month target, stated once with its method; the intrinsic value once as a cross-check), rounding to whole currency units and whole percents (no penny targets, no single-basis-point spreads), formulas only when the supplied inputs reproduce the result, a stressed bear case with the operating stress behind it and an explicit upside-to-downside skew, and the bridge from the current metric through the operating lever to the normalised run-rate before any "normalised" claim.
- The writer's inputs gained a forward-estimates block: management's latest guidance with its date and source (earnings release or call), consensus, the house bear / base / bull estimates and whether the DCF ran on them; the quant anchors gained PRIMARY ANCHOR, CROSS-CHECK ONLY, RISK-REWARD (bear and bull from the scenario targets, the skew ratio, an under-stressed-bear call-out) and a "Rounded for prose" line the number guard accepts.

### Guidance-to-forecast engine (owner's five principles, 2026-10-03)
- `src/agents/analysis/guidance_forecast.py`, deterministic. From the guidance block (FY+1, FY+2 and a new `medium_term_target` the research extractor now captures: a 2029 EPS workplan, a margin target, a breakeven year) and the audited history it builds the intermediate years a flat rate misses: (1) the target year is back-solved into implied EBIT, net income, tax and non-operating lines and the endpoints are checked for compatibility; a guided CAGR more than 500bp above the market's is flagged for share capture, capacity or M&A; (2) the valuation profile routes to one of four archetypes whose curve shapes the margin path — regulatory lag (S-curve, year-1 lag), fixed-asset operating leverage (convex J-curve), retail rollout (linear with pre-opening drag), platform scale (linear) — with a generic fallback; (3) the three statements: working-capital absorption from the history's intensity, capex as maintenance (D&A) plus growth (capital intensity times new revenue), D&A rolled forward, tax and interest from the history, net income, EPS and unlevered FCF per year; (4) after the target year growth fades linearly to the engine's terminal rate over four years; terminal ROIC (at least WACC + 200bp) gives the reinvestment identity and the implied exit multiple beside the engine's own terminal; (5) five invariants recorded with each run: operating jaws, cash conversion 60–120% of net income, share-count integrity against the buyback history, terminal multiple against the mid-cycle peer median, and the no-residue rule (held by the PM prompt).
- The DCF leg runs on the forecast's growth AND FCF-margin schedules (one projection, no display-only value); the FY+1/FY+2 channel remains the fallback. The forecast is published at `dcf_range[t].guidance_forecast`, shown under the guidance card on the web (archetype, path, year table, back-solve, terminal, checks), in the PDF block and on the Excel Guidance tab, and the PM writer's forward block names the archetype, the target-year path and any failed check. Constants PROPOSED in `valuation_constants.json["guidance_forecast"]`.
- On the Molina shape (FY2026 −7.5% then +10.7%, EBIT margin 2.9% → 5%, 2029 EPS $25 workplan): archetype I, a four-year S-curve, EPS $15.5 → $21.9 → $26.6 → $25.0 (the guided EPS implies 59m shares, inside the buyback history), steady-state cash conversion 1.03x, and an implied exit multiple of 12.1x against a 9.0x peer median, flagged.

### The DCF cash flow is unlevered (SBUX review, A2; owner, 2026-10-03)
- Reported free cash flow is after interest. Discounting it at WACC and then subtracting net debt counted the debt twice. The DCF base is now unlevered owner-earnings FCF: owner-earnings FCF plus after-tax interest, the tax being each year's effective rate on pre-tax profit (bounded 10–35%, 21% where the statements give no usable rate). The series is FX-converted with the other money fields, the Tier-1 first-pass margin uses the same basis, and the forward flag "Unlevered basis: …" names both margins and the year count.
- Balance-sheet intermediaries (Banks, Insurance, Fee financials) and the property family (NAV / P/B / AFFO legs) keep the levered basis: interest is their cost of goods or their leverage is the asset. Fewer than three years of the series also keeps the levered basis, and the flag says which basis priced the name.

### Guidance-derived estimates price the forward multiples; overrides carry forward; the PM text can be rewritten (owner, 2026-10-03)
- "Why is the valuation agent always running DCF, especially the management guidance to estimates?" Because the forecast engine produced schedules and only the DCF consumed a schedule; the forward multiples priced consensus NTM EPS and EBITDA, so on a multiples-weighted profile (SBUX's Restaurants) the agent's estimates changed nothing. Now, per scenario, the forward legs price on the guidance-derived FY+1 estimates: EPS from the research's estimate (else the forecast's year-1 EPS), EBITDA from FY+1 revenue × the FY+1 margin (else the forecast's EBIT + D&A), revenue and EBIT from the forecast. Consensus stays wherever no estimate exists and is kept beside the estimate on every leg (`consensus_value`); the leg label names the source; a flag lists the basis. Trailing legs (FCF yield, P/B) do not move on a forward estimate. The PM's inputs carry a "Forward multiples basis" line (estimate vs consensus, the gap in percent) and the prompt tells the writer to say so once.
- The estimate workbench re-prices the same forward legs on the user's FY+1 EPS or margin, scaled against the agent's own FY+1 figure so a leg keeps its basis and moves only for the user's change; the preview lists each leg's metric and value, agent → user. Terminal ROIC is an engine override. A DCF the agent's run dropped (it did not price) is re-weighted from the profile's intended weights when the user's numbers make it price.
- A saved override is carried into the ticker's next run: its numbers replace the research's in the guidance block (and are the block where research gave none), its engine inputs reach the forecast, its WACC or terminal growth replace the engine's; the run flags "USER ESTIMATES CARRIED FORWARD" and the PM inputs name the user's estimates. The Model Accuracy page lists the latest carried override per ticker with a revoke; `ESTIMATE_OVERRIDE_CARRY=off` stops the carry without touching the rows. Persistence moved to `src/data/estimate_override_store.py`, shared by the web service and the pipeline.
- The report header says when the target reflects an override the PM text predates; "Rewrite PM rationale on my figures" (one model call, on request) reruns the portfolio manager on the overridden run and the new rationale and headline ride the override record, applied on read, with the agent's text kept beside them. Bear and bull forecasts (steps, rows, checks) are published beside the base one and the forecast card toggles between them.
- Owner, 2026-10-03: the accuracy tracker scores the agent's estimates, never a user's. A read-time override never reaches the archive rows the tracker reads (`ticker_signals`, the raw `web_runs` blob for recaps and the target history), and a run the pipeline built on a carried user override is excluded from scoring by its `estimate_override_carried` marker.

### Three-statement forecast FY+1E–FY+5E on the agent's estimates, with the reconciliation suite (owner, 2026-10-03)
- The check the owner asked for: the forecast engine supplies the operating lines (revenue path, EBIT margin path, D&A, capex, working-capital absorption, tax, base-year interest). A full model also needs gross margin, SBC, the interest rate on debt and on cash, the payout ratio, buybacks and a buyback price, receivable / inventory / payable days, a debt policy, a minimum cash level, minority share of earnings and M&A. `three_statement.assumptions_from_history` takes each from the audited filings with its source named (history, latest year, default, assumption, missing) and `coverage()` prints the list; the opening balance sheet is the latest audited year with residual "other" lines so it balances as filed.
- `three_statement.build` follows the owner's flow: income statement (revenue & EBIT) → supporting schedules (working capital in days, PP&E roll with capex and D&A, debt with a revolver that only holds the minimum cash) → cash flow (net change in cash, FCF) → balance sheet (cash, PP&E, debt, retained earnings; assets = liabilities + equity). Retained earnings and other equity are carried separately so the owner's third assertion reads exactly as written.
- The reconciliation suite runs on every forecast column before anything prints: (1) |assets − liabilities − equity| < 0.01, (2) cash ending = beginning + CFS net change, (3) retained earnings ending = prior + net income − dividends, (4) net PP&E = prior + capex − depreciation, (5) CFS working-capital change = −(Δ current assets ex cash − Δ current liabilities ex short debt). A failed assertion withholds the statements; the trace (year, assertion, both sides, difference) prints instead, on the Model tab and in the PDF, and the run flags it.
- Excel: a Model tab with the opening column as filed, the assumptions block in blue, and the four blocks as live formulas, plus a fifth block with the five assertions as OK / FAIL per column and a suite result. PDF: the four tables and the suite under the forecast block. Web: the workbench exposes gross margin, SBC, interest rate, payout and buybacks as overrides; a save rebuilds the statements with everything else. Balance-sheet families (banks, insurers, property) are skipped with the reason: they need an earnings-and-capital model, not a working-capital roll.

### Steps two and three: family-specific guidance and the bank / insurer earnings-and-capital model (owner, 2026-10-03)
- The research extractor's guidance schema gains `family_metrics`: for a bank, loan growth, net interest margin, fee growth, cost-to-income, credit cost (bps), payout, CET1 target and an ROE target year; for an insurer, premium growth, combined ratio, investment yield and payout. The normaliser carries them as decimals (bps as numbers) and null for any other company.
- `src/agents/analysis/bank_model.py`: the earnings-and-capital model. Balance-sheet drivers (asset growth, NIM on average earning assets, loans) → income statement (NII + fees − cost-to-income × income − credit cost × loans − tax) → capital (retained earnings build CET1 against the target; dividends are cut before the ratio breaches; buybacks only from headroom) → per share (EPS, DPS, BVPS, tangible BVPS, ROE, RoTE). Each assumption comes from the first source that has it: family guidance → the bank-metrics extraction → the audited line items → a PROPOSED default, with the source named. Scenario bands are PROPOSED constants (bear NIM −10bp, credit cost ×1.5, cost-to-income +2pp; bull the mirror). An insurer variant runs on premiums, the combined ratio and the investment yield on the float. Its own five-assertion suite (equity roll, BVPS identity, CET1 identity, earnings identity, CET1 at or above target) gates the output.
- The legs read the model: the GGM (P/B) leg's RoTE is the model's steady-state RoTE on the guided drivers (ranked above the research's point target) and its book is the model's FY+1 book value per share, both named in the provenance; the forward P/E prices the model's FY+1 EPS. The working-capital roll no longer runs for a bank; the bank model is published as the family's statements (web context, Excel Model tab in a bank layout with every assumption's source, PDF block), with bear and bull beside the base. The workbench exposes the drivers (loan growth, NIM, fee growth, cost-to-income, credit cost, CET1 target, payout; premium growth, combined ratio, investment yield) and a save re-prices the GGM and forward P/E legs on the user's model.
- The model prices the legs only when management's own drivers built it (family guidance in the research); otherwise it is a cross-check beside the research's target and the flag says so, with a calibration note (FY+1 net income against the latest audited year, ±20%). Without the guard a line-item NIM of 0.74% re-priced Standard Chartered's GGM and moved its value 17%. Goldens unchanged (no fixture carries family guidance); a replay with injected bank guidance pins that the GGM then reads the model's RoTE and FY+1 book.
- Owner, 2026-10-03 (Anta 02020.HK): the estimates were on the Model tab but the IS, BS and CFS tabs showed FY2026E onward empty. The three statement tabs now carry FY+1E..FY+5E columns linked line by line to the Model tab (a bank's IS and BS link to the earnings-and-capital model; its CFS has no forecast), the Model tab sits directly after CFS, and a withheld model writes its reason into the forecast columns instead of leaving blanks.
- The IS tab's FY+1E..FY+5E are now filled on the reported rows themselves (revenue, COGS, EBIT, D&A, interest, pre-tax, tax, net income, shares link to the Model tab; growth, margins, EBITDA, tax rate, EPS extend the tab's own formulas), not in a block below them.
- PDF: the Financial Statements page carries FY+1E..FY+5E beside the four actual years in the same Growth & Margins, Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow tables (full width when forecasts are present; expenses positive and outflows negative as the reported rows print them; growth, margin and ROE rows extend), with the reconciliation suite beneath and a source line naming the forecast's basis. The standalone three-statement block under the valuation summary remains only for a bank or insurer model and for a withheld (failed-suite) model, where the reason prints.
