## 2026-10-03 — v2.23.0: One SOTP Format, the Fast Research Tier, and the Guidance → Estimates Channel

### Executive Summary
Every sum-of-the-parts leg now publishes in the one analyst format (segment table, then the bridge to a per-share figure). Deep research moved to the DashScope flash tier for speed, with the thinking it does spend pointed at one job: translating management guidance into the bear / base / bull estimates a valuation runs on. Those estimates set the DCF's first two years per scenario and are shown on the web, in the PDF and in the Excel model, for every sector and profile.

### One format for every SOTP leg
- 71038af: SOTP (analyst), SOTP (segments), SOTP / NAV (look-through), SOTP (published) and SOTP (FRE + carry) all render as the analyst card: valuation sentence, segment table (forward revenue, earnings, method, multiple, value, share), then associates, net cash, holdco discount and the per-share figure. One `sotp_breakdown` block per run, built from whichever SOTP leg carries weight, with that leg's method and weight in the title; the look-through and published legs now trace their own tables so the block is their numbers. The segment-only panel and PDF block are retired; archived runs' legacy blocks are adapted on read.

### The fast research tier (objective i)
- `qwen3.8-flash` is the research default for Tier-1 search, synthesis and every extractor (the owner's "Qwen3.8-Flash-Next"; the id that resolves on this account carries no suffix). `DEEP_RESEARCH_MODEL` and `DEEP_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS_MODEL` still override — production sets both, so the switch takes effect there only when those variables change.
- The flash tier thinks by default on the Anthropic-compatible endpoint: with no thinking parameter and a 4,000-token cap it returned a thinking block and no text. Every client deep research builds now carries a thinking policy (off unless a budget is asked for), and the Tier-1 search runs with thinking off under the "pro" strategy (the flash tier refuses "agent"): measured 5 s against 104 s for one guidance question. `DEEP_RESEARCH_SEARCH_STRATEGY` and `DEEP_RESEARCH_SEARCH_THINKING=on` restore the old behaviour.
- Report length: a compact budget (3,000 words of prose outside the machine-read blocks, per-section caps, no introductions) and a 12,000-token output cap on the flash tier. `DEEP_RESEARCH_LENGTH=full` and `DEEP_RESEARCH_REPORT_MAX_TOKENS` restore the long form.

### Guidance → estimates (objective ii)
- The search sequence leads with guidance: the latest results release and its ranges, the call's commentary and medium-term targets, consensus for the comparison. A new section 2G carries a machine-readable GUIDANCE_BLOCK (what management guided, consensus, the prior-year actual), the arithmetic of what it implies, management's record on its own guidance, and the analyst's recommended FY+1 / FY+2 revenue growth, margin and EPS for bear / base / bull with a confidence. Both section parsers (live and archive) read 2A–2G.
- A universal `guidance_estimates` extractor runs over 2G with a thinking budget (6,000 tokens; the only extractor that thinks) and returns rates as decimals, so the engine needs no currency or scale conversion. Persisted extractor sets from before 2G are reused for the extractors they have; only the missing one runs.
- The DCF's guidance channel: years 1–2 of each scenario's growth come from that scenario's estimate (FY+1, then FY+2), a three-year fade lands on the engine's own path by year 6, and the terminal is untouched. LOW-confidence estimates are shown but not channelled; `GUIDANCE_CHANNEL=off` disables. Constants are PROPOSED in `valuation_constants.json["guidance_channel"]` for the owner's review. This closes the gap where guided growth was overridden by analyst dispersion bands before the scenarios ran.

### Estimates on every surface (objective iii)
- Web (desktop and mobile paths): a "Management guidance → estimates" card beside the SOTP card — guidance as stated, consensus, the model's bear / base / bull, the base DCF's growth path, and whether it was applied or why not.
- PDF: the same block after the SOTP block in the Valuation Summary. Excel: a "Guidance" tab with the same figures and the year-by-year growth path the base DCF ran on.

### The brief is the research deliverable; the research text is context
- Owner, 2026-10-03: the Industry Intelligence Brief keeps its depth and coverage (eight to twelve assertion-headed bullets over competitive position, growth durability, margins, balance sheet, guidance vs consensus and the 2G estimates, the thesis risk). The Section 2 text behind it is working context for the brief and the extractors and is no longer printed: the deep research panel and sub-tab are removed from the desktop and mobile report paths, and the PDF already printed the brief.
- Citations survive the change: footnote numbers are global across the report, and the brief's [n] markers are resolved from the sections' REFERENCES blocks into `industry_footnotes` (source, date, URL), rendered as a References list under the brief on the web and in the PDF. A marker with no entry stays visible as unresolved rather than vanishing.
- The flash tier's first live run wrote a textual tool call and stopped (173 characters); the DashScope path now gets a native-search prompt (no tool to call) and one retry with thinking on when Tier 1 comes back degenerate.

### The PM write-up, to an institutional standard
- Owner's critique (2026-10-03), five findings, each now a rule in the rationale prompt: no engine residue (flags, gates, legs and runs are translated into operating reality), one price anchor (the 12-month target, stated once with its method; the intrinsic value once as a cross-check), rounding to whole currency units and whole percents (no penny targets, no single-basis-point spreads), formulas only when the supplied inputs reproduce the result, a stressed bear case with the operating stress behind it and an explicit upside-to-downside skew, and the bridge from the current metric through the operating lever to the normalised run-rate before any "normalised" claim.
- The writer's inputs gained a forward-estimates block: management's latest guidance with its date and source (earnings release or call), consensus, the house bear / base / bull estimates and whether the DCF ran on them; the quant anchors gained PRIMARY ANCHOR, CROSS-CHECK ONLY, RISK-REWARD (bear and bull from the scenario targets, the skew ratio, an under-stressed-bear call-out) and a "Rounded for prose" line the number guard accepts.

### Wave 10 renewables: the PPA project-finance DCF (owner methodology)
- The IPP profile's "PPA-backed DCF" anchor was the core DCF under another name. It now computes the owner's method on an accepted, review-gated `ppa` input (operating capacity and generation, contracted share, remaining term, PPA price or contracted revenue, escalator, merchant price, opex, project debt and its cost, tax equity, tax credits, technology mix): P50 generation with degradation; the contracted window at the escalating PPA; the uncontracted share and every year after the term at a capture-adjusted merchant price; opex, maintenance reserves and cash tax net of credits; no terminal value past the asset life; FCFF discounted in two pieces (contracted at the profile WACC, merchant at WACC plus a premium); FCFE through debt sculpted to the contract term and discounted at the cost of equity, preferred when the input carries the project debt. Without an accepted input the leg stays the core DCF and the report says so (GATE_PPA_INPUT).
